Creative Mind

I’m Graciela, a computer science and design studies student at DePauw University, graduating in May 2028. I started out as a writer, fascinated by how a story can pull someone into another world. When I discovered coding and game development, I realized I could build worlds people step into and interact with. Now I bring that storyteller’s eye to UI/UX design, creating experiences that feel intuitive, engaging, and human.
